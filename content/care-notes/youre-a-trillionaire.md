# You’re a trillionaire

*Healing Images · An academic poem from OpenHealthcare.org*

Your body contains trillions of human cells. This poem pairs that scientific scale with a quieter question: how might wonder change the way we regard ourselves? [1,2]

![A field of blue and turquoise cells surrounds a smiling golden cell. Text reads: You’re a trillionaire. Your body is made of trillions of cells. OpenHealthcare.org.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/healcare/openhealthcare-data/main/assets/healing-images/images/youre-a-trillionaire.webp)

*A Universe of Living Cells · AI-generated artwork from the Healing Images collection.*

## Observation

Hold out your hand.  
A familiar outline.  
Within that small horizon,  
life exceeds the scale  
at which we usually look.

## Census

To approach the number,  
researchers gathered measurements—  
tissue by tissue,  
type by type—  
until the familiar body  
opened into trillions. [1]

## Proportion

Blood cells dominate the count. [1,2]  
Mass tells another story:  
muscle and fat hold much  
of the cellular weight. [1]  
Even within one body,  
abundance has more than one measure.

## Time

Some cells are replaced quickly;  
others remain for years. [3]  
A living body keeps  
more than one kind of time.

## A different kind of wealth

Call it a fortune, if you like:  
a wealth beneath the visible,  
a gathering too vast  
to meet one cell at a time.

**You’re a trillionaire.**  
The unit is life.

## Beyond the measurement

Yet the count is not a price.  
You are worthy of care  
before the first number  
is written down.

On the page, one golden cell smiles.  
That smile belongs to art.  
The tenderness it asks of us  
can belong to the way  
we meet ourselves.

## Research notes

**1. How many cells?** Hatton and colleagues estimated approximately 36 trillion cells for a 70 kg adult male reference body, 28 trillion for a 60 kg adult female reference body, and 17 trillion for a 32 kg, 10-year-old child. Their 2023 synthesis drew on more than 1,500 published sources. These are model-based estimates, not individualized measurements or universal totals. Blood cells dominate cell number; muscle and fat dominate cellular biomass. [1]

**2. Why say trillions rather than one exact number?** Sender, Fuchs and Milo estimated about 30 trillion human cells in a 70 kg reference adult male in 2016. Different studies use different inputs and counting conventions; both support the scale of trillions. The totals discussed here concern human cells, not an added bacterial-cell tally. [2]

**3. What does renewal mean?** NIH’s National Institute of General Medical Sciences describes widely differing cell lifetimes: many blood and intestinal-lining cells turn over quickly, while other cells persist for years or longer. This does not mean the whole body renews on a single timetable. [3]

*“Trillionaire” is a poetic metaphor for cellular abundance. The smiling cell is artistic personification. Neither is a measure of health, human worth, or a promise of healing.*
