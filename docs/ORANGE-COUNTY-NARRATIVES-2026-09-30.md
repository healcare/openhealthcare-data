# Orange County profile narratives — research draft

Prepared September 30, 2026. Existing eight-person roster; permanent IDs and URLs preserved. Source-stated biographies, not independent current credential verification or clinician endorsement.

## Amy Bandy, DO

Amy Bandy describes discovering her interest in surgery during medical-school rotations, after initially considering pediatrics. Her professional biography traces general surgery training at Harbor-UCLA and plastic and reconstructive surgery residency at Wayne State University. In her Newport Beach practice, she encourages patients to arrive with questions and discuss what they hope to change. That emphasis on conversation gives her profile a personal, practical focus.

[Official biography](https://www.drbandy.com/about/dr-amy-bandy/) · [OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/amybandy)

## Sanjay Grover, MD

Sanjay Grover’s training took him through three California universities: UCLA for undergraduate study, UC San Diego for medicine, and Stanford for general and plastic surgery residencies. His practice biography describes a further fellowship in aesthetic and oculoplastic surgery in Atlanta. His Newport Beach work spans the face, nose, breast, and body, with an emphasis on time spent understanding each patient’s goals.

[Official biography](https://www.drgrover.com/our-practice/dr-grover/) · [OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/sanjaygrover)

## Kevin Sadati, DO

Kevin Sadati’s professional story connects medicine with painting and sculpture. His biography describes studying science and the humanities at UC Berkeley, followed by osteopathic medicine at Midwestern University. While training in Philadelphia, he also attended evening art classes. Today, his practice describes a focus on facial rejuvenation and teaching surgical techniques, with artistic composition informing how he thinks about facial form.

[Official biography](https://galleryofcosmeticsurgery.com/teams/dr-kevin-sadati/) · [OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/kevinsadati)

## Joseph T. Cruise, MD

Joseph Cruise’s biography begins in Michigan, where he completed undergraduate and medical studies at Wayne State University. Alongside his cosmetic practice, it describes earlier reconstructive work with burn patients. His approach to consultations emphasizes an honest discussion of what surgery can and cannot achieve, sometimes using computer imaging to illustrate possibilities. His Newport Beach practice includes gynecomastia, breast surgery, and facial procedures.

[Official biography](https://orangecountycosmeticsurgery.com/about/about-us/) · [OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/josephcruise)

## Juris Bunkis, MD

Juris Bunkis is introduced by Orange County Plastic Surgery as its founder and medical director. The practice describes facial rejuvenation as a particular interest, alongside breast surgery and body contouring. His biography also connects his work with teaching and community service. The practice now lists its office in the CosmetiCare building in Corona del Mar, part of the Newport Beach area.

[Official biography](https://orangecountyplasticsurgery.com/) · [OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/jurisbunkis)

## Michael A. Bain, MD

A Newport Beach native, Michael Bain studied at UCLA before earning medical and medical-science degrees at Hahnemann University, according to his biography. His practice combines aesthetic and reconstructive procedures for the face, breast, and body. Its consultation description puts individual goals, available options, and recovery planning at the center of the discussion, giving patients practical questions to explore before choosing a procedure.

[Official biography](https://www.drbain.com/about) · [OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/michaelbain)

## Ali Sepehr, MD

Ali Sepehr describes a practice devoted to facial plastic surgery. His professional biography follows a California education at UCLA and residency training at UC Irvine with a facial plastic and reconstructive surgery fellowship at the University of Toronto. It also describes teaching and published research. For prospective patients, the practice frames consultation as a chance to discuss goals, understand procedures, and ask about training and recovery.

[Official biography](https://www.orangecountyfacialplasticsurgery.com/about/surgeon-ali-sepehr/) · [OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/alisepehr)

## Ali Sajjadian, MD

Ali Sajjadian’s biography brings together surgical practice, teaching, and research. It describes medical education in Philadelphia, plastic surgery training at the University of Pittsburgh, and a facial plastic surgery fellowship at Emory. Rhinoplasty and revision rhinoplasty feature prominently in his Newport Beach practice. His stated approach begins with listening to a patient’s goals and developing an individual plan through that conversation.

[Official biography](https://www.drsajjadian.com/about/ali-sajjadian-md/) · [OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/alisajjadian)
