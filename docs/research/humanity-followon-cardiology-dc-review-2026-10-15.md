## Retrieval and freshness findings useful at scale
1. **Filename does not establish format.** Murphy's .png and Bonior's .jpg URLs return decodable WebP. Import must inspect bytes, use suitable local extension/content type, and retain exact original source URLs.
2. **Search extraction can lag live markup.** Murphy's extracted page linked a 2020 headshot; inspected live HTML exposed a 2026 headshot. Both are source observations, not proof one image is unauthorized. Only the chosen current live asset was visually approved in this packet.
3. **Old publication wording is not current status.** Smith's undated biography calls a July 2024 book upcoming and differs on newsletter cadence. Narula's June article anticipates an award ceremony. Exclude stale temporal claims rather than silently updating them.
4. **Provider name variations require reconciliation.** Tara Narula Cangello and Anuradha/Anu Lala-Trindade are source-exposed variants; do not assign multiple identities or invent unsupported route distinctions.
5. **Geography is independent of narrative/portrait quality.** Bonior may fit a DC telehealth directory only under its actual scope. Otherwise retain a prepared research candidate without adding misleading membership or counting it.
6. **Professional page access is not current capacity.** Intake links and advertised video visits are not successful bookings. Keep fee, insurance and location scope dated and source-stated.
7. **Research readiness is not patient-visible delivery.** These seven can only advance the milestone after accepted full narrative, usable citations and a decoded local preview are delivered on actual patient pages under existing preservation tests.
8. **Source quality differs by claim.** An institutional biography can support a public role or story without proving current license, insurance reimbursement or clinical outcomes.
