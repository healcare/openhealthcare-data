# Profile content and research boundary
User direction, 2026-10-10. Applies to Manhattan mental health and future cohorts.

## Purpose
GitHub or a future text repository should deliver results of analysis: clean, accessible, usable healthcare data with claim-level evidence and accurately described verification. It should also preserve useful research, separately from publishable profile content.
The Manhattan research master is a research archive, not a public-page content packet. Its inclusion does not instruct publishing every lead, uncertainty or research prompt.

## Public-facing primary content
Show concise original narrative, meaningful human details, care/access facts, source-linked photo previews and useful media. Use evidence-supported claims with current scope and provenance. Distinguish independently verified facts from official source statements; never label all official claims independently verified.
Public-facing status must distinguish unknown availability from explicit closure, clinical services from coaching/education, and organization policy from provider participation.
Maintain stable identity and canonical links, accessibility, and existing human-review/indexing rules.

## Optional collapsed public evidence and corrections
A clearly labelled expandable section can expose source URLs, observation dates, verification method, meaningful limitations and selected corrections. Keep important access conflicts visible near the affected fact; essential warnings must not exist only inside a collapsed section.
An outdated phone number may be displayed struck through only if evidence establishes that it is outdated. Otherwise show a source discrepancy without selecting a winner.
Never make superseded numbers callable or place them in primary contact links, current structured contact data or search summaries.
Corrected facts should retain a short rationale and evidence where useful to readers or AI agents. Include only relevant, proportionate research notes, not every failed search.
Expandable content must remain accessible and retrievable as text, with explicit current versus historical status. Make it easy for agents to reuse the evidence without repeating research.

## Repository-only research archive
Preserve discovery leads, failed retrievals, attribution investigations, raw research batches, prompts, alternative interpretations and fuller research chronology here. These are not automatically eligible for public display.
Public GitHub is still publicly accessible: do not add private patient information, secrets or material that should remain confidential.

## Deliverable distinction
Maintain a clear distinction between:
1. Profile-ready content and current access facts, each linked to supporting evidence and scope.
2. Selected public evidence/correction notes.
3. Repository-only research and unresolved leads.
Use archive material to develop content packets; do not copy archive prose wholesale onto profiles or list unverified leads as available clinicians.

## Immediate Manhattan mental-health application
Read research/manhattan-mental-health-research-master-2026-10-10.md as background only. Use research/manhattan-mental-health-pilot-access-review-2026-10-10.md for supported corrections and access findings, with its explicit limitations.
Develop polished profiles only from eligible evidence. Keep educators without verified Manhattan intake out of local available-care listings. Keep coaching explicit. Do not publish research prompts or failed-search narration in profile bodies.
