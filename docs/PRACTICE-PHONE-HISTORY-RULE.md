# Practice phone numbers and dated phone history

Adopted November 3, 2026 by owner instruction. This is the shared rule for **all future directory creation and approved phone/contact updates**, including pediatric dentistry. It extends the rule first recorded for the Portland pediatric dentist profiles. Apply it within each approved batch; adoption alone does not authorize rewriting other records or changing publication, indexing, review or claim states.

## Rule

> Keep current practice office numbers separate from dated historical observations. Link only numbers the current source lists, label them untested, and show archived numbers as non-clickable history. Do not assign a practice-wide line to an individual clinician, infer why a number changed, or claim the archive proves a scam or authenticates today's number.

A source check, an archive observation and an actual phone test are separate evidence events. A source-listed number may be shown with its source/date and an explicit **not call-tested** label. No call is required merely to display an approved source-stated practice number, and this rule does not authorize making calls.

## Research handoff

Include the following in the existing approved packet or a linked evidence note. These are evidence requirements, not a new database schema.

| Item | Required evidence and limits |
| --- | --- |
| Current listing | Exact number as listed, practice/office scope, named source and exact URL, and the date the source was observed. Keep a page's stated date separate from the retrieval date. |
| Association | Distinguish practice-wide, office-specific and clinician-specific contact information. State when clinician-to-office or clinician-to-number associations are unknown. |
| Phone-test status | Default to not call-tested. If a separately authorized test actually occurred, retain its date, scoped result and limitations; a successful connection does not prove appointments, insurance acceptance or every clinician's availability. |
| Dated history | For each retained observation, keep the listed number and office label, exact archive/source URL, capture or document date, and retrieval date. Identify the number of samples reviewed and material gaps. |
| Changes and conflicts | Retain earlier evidence and unresolved alternatives. Report the observed last-old/first-new samples without inventing the actual transition date, cause, ownership or fraud findings. Archives do not authenticate a current line. |
| Apply/hold instructions | Name the stable affected IDs, approved current presentation, historical plain-text content, unsupported structured fields and unresolved associations. Preserve existing stronger evidence and all unrelated holds. |

When no archive history has been examined or no usable capture exists, say so. Missing history is not proof that a number never changed. Reuse retained observations with their dates; this policy does not require an exhaustive archive search or repeated discovery for every batch. Escalate a specific unresolved conflict to the research workflow rather than guessing.

## Replit integration

- Render approved current practice/office contact information in a clearly labeled shared section. Dial links must identify the relevant practice or office and retain the source-stated, not-call-tested status when no call test exists.
- Render historical numbers as plain text with dated citations. They must not become dial links or be promoted into current contact fields, structured data, share metadata or other current-contact outputs.
- Do not copy a shared practice-wide number into an individual clinician's phone field by inference. Keep unsupported clinician phone fields unset and office assignments empty unless the supplied evidence explicitly supports the association. Preserve valid existing, separately supported contact evidence.
- Preserve permanent IDs, routes, citations, dated observations, portraits, claim/review/indexing states and clinical or identity holds. Phone history does not establish licensure, specialty credentials, office staffing, rights clearance or human review.
- Consume the pinned research handoff without repeating source discovery, calling the practice or simulating verification. Return a concrete evidence exception to research if the current source, supplied scope or implementation contract conflicts.

## Proportionate acceptance and result receipt

For a batch that changes phone presentation, inspect the affected profiles on mobile and desktop. Check source/office labels, accessible current dial links, the not-call-tested disclosure, historical numbers remaining non-clickable, citation destinations, keyboard focus, layout and the existing scoped privacy assertions. Check provider/API/structured outputs for accidental promotion of shared or historical numbers and preserve existing data guards.

Use the application's existing meaningful checks for the affected surface; do not add a new full-suite requirement for documentation-only adoption. Record passes, failures, timeouts and unrun checks separately. Technical tests validate rendering and preservation, not whether a phone is authentic or currently answered.

The result receipt names the data-policy/packet pin, application revision, affected IDs, applied/held fields, actual tests and unresolved evidence. A local commit, GitHub backup, preview, recovery check and production publication are separate outcomes.
