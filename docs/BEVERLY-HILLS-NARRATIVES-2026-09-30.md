## Gabriel Chiu, DO

Gabriel Chiu brings an interest in visual detail to both surgery and photography. His practice biography describes a collaborative consultation: time to discuss concerns, ask questions, and decide how to proceed. His training took him from microbiology and immunology at UC Berkeley to osteopathic medicine at Western University and a plastic and reconstructive surgery fellowship. Away from medicine, photography remains one of his interests.

[Official biography](https://www.beverlyhillsplasticsurgeryinc.com/surgeon/) · [Existing OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/gabrielchiu)

## Michael K. Obeng, MD

Michael K. Obeng’s professional story spans aesthetic surgery and reconstructive work. His MiKO biography describes breast and body procedures alongside complex reconstruction. It also identifies him as the founder of RESTORE Worldwide, an organization providing donated reconstructive surgery. That work gives his profile a second point of focus: surgical care for people affected by congenital conditions and injuries, beyond his Beverly Hills practice.

[Official biography](https://www.mikoplasticsurgery.com/dr-michael-k-obeng) · [Existing OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/michaelobeng)

## Payam Jarrah-Nejad, MD

Known professionally as Dr. J, Payam Jarrah-Nejad describes communication as central to his approach: patients should understand a procedure and what recovery involves. His biography traces a route through general surgery, a hand surgery fellowship at Beth Israel Medical Center, and plastic surgery training at Wayne State University. It also describes volunteer work with Operation Smile and fluency in English and Farsi.

[Official biography](https://drjplasticsurgery.com/beverly-hills-plastic-surgeon/) · [Existing OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/drjplasticsurgery)

## Malcolm Lesavoy, MD

Teaching and reconstructive surgery run through Malcolm Lesavoy’s professional biography. His published career summary records leadership of plastic and reconstructive surgery at Harbor-UCLA from 1976 through 1999, following training at the University of Chicago and University of Miami. His Beverly Hills practice describes face, breast, and body procedures. The biography also highlights volunteer work with children and an interest in animal welfare.

[Official biography](https://lesavoyplasticsurgery.com/about/) · [Existing OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/malcolmlesavoy)

## Rady Rahban, MD

Rady Rahban makes patient education a visible part of his practice, including through his Plastic Surgery Uncensored podcast. His biography traces his education from biochemistry at UCLA to medical school at UC San Diego and surgical training at USC. It also describes reconstructive volunteer work in Latin America. Beyond the operating room, he lists photography, cycling, and snowboarding among his interests.

[Official biography](https://www.radyrahban.com/our-practice/dr-rady-rahban/) · [Existing OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/radyrahban)

## Daniel Barrett, MD, MHA, MS

Daniel Barrett’s practice presents recovery and scar management as part of the conversation about aesthetic surgery. His published training history combines medicine, physiology, and health administration, followed by a six-year plastic and reconstructive surgery residency at USC. His Beverly Hills work includes face, breast, and body procedures. The practice also describes Barrett Gives Back, a program offering selected reconstructive and post-weight-loss procedures without charge.

[Official biography](https://www.drdanielbarrett.com/dr-barrett) · [Existing OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/danielbarrett)

## Garth Fisher, MD

Garth Fisher’s biography connects a Beverly Hills surgical practice with a long-running interest in public education. Alongside cosmetic procedures for the face, nose, breast, and body, it describes his role on Extreme Makeover and the creation of The Naked Truth About Plastic Surgery video series. His path began in Mississippi, followed by medical school there and surgical training at UCLA.

[Official biography](https://www.garthfisher.com/about-dr-fisher/meet-dr-fisher/) · [Existing OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/garthfisher)

## Daniel Gould, MD, PhD

Daniel Gould’s published biography brings medicine and bioengineering into the same professional story. It lists an MD from Baylor College of Medicine, a bioengineering PhD from Rice University, and plastic surgery residency at USC. His Beverly Hills practice describes an emphasis on facial and neck surgery alongside breast and body procedures. Teaching, mentoring, and research are also part of the career described on his official profile.

[Official biography](https://drgouldplasticsurgery.com/about/dr-daniel-gould/) · [Existing OpenHealthcare profile](https://openhealthcare.org/danielgould)
