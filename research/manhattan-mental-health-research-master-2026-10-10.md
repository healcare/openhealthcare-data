## Purpose and contribution
Explore a Manhattan mental-health pilot that extends OpenHealthcare's narrative profiles into relationships, anxiety, parenting and emotional wellbeing. Combine practical access and public education. Prominence, books and media do not establish local clinical availability or quality.
Use Chiu's profile baseline: concise overview, source-supported human detail, readable original story, meaningful sections and accessible external media cards. Avoid prestige filler, rankings and unsupported best-for recommendations.
Service types must distinguish psychologists, psychotherapists, psychiatrists, coaching and educational resources. Clinical location and eligibility matter independently of media fame.

## Import and presentation decisions
Prepare review pilot, not deployment. New entries non-indexable and pending human review under contribution contract.
Suggested browsing distinctions: Find care, Affordable care, Learn from experts. One person may have different clinical and education sections without duplicated identity records.
Access fields: service type, stated availability and date, intake URL/phone, treatment location, fees/insurance products, age groups, telehealth jurisdiction, language and accommodation evidence. Unknown is separate from closed. Coaching is separate from clinical psychotherapy.
No third-party players before click; media opens externally in new window with accessible labeling.
Ordinary source-linked image previews follow the user's Chiu policy; derivative trading-card use remains separately assessed. No portraits have been inspected for this new mental-health cohort in our work so far.
