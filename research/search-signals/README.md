# Open search signals

This plain-text research layer preserves useful leads, explicit participation and nonparticipation, ambiguous findings, unsuccessful searches and corrections. It helps patients and future agents avoid wasted calls and repeated searches. It is an extra evidence-discovery source, not an automatic directory feed.

## Lightweight entries

Use dated topic logs in this folder. Each entry needs only a subject, geographic/entity scope, source URL (or actual query when no URL returned), brief observation or short excerpt, access method, date, uncertainty and next step. Valid evidence labels include snippet-only, page-opened/source-stated, historical, conflicting, retrieval-failed and superseded. Preserve reported dates separately from our retrieval dates. Never invent a URL or silently upgrade an observation.

Unstructured prose is welcome. Corrections can reference and supersede previous entries rather than erase their history. A failed retrieval and a query with no matches are different outcomes. Search snippets, aggregator records and provider statements are useful signals with different evidentiary weight. An attributable signal is worth saving even when it is not sufficient for a public badge.

Record individual clinicians, organizations and service locations separately. Do not inherit one office's participation across a group without scoped evidence. Do not merge clinicians or organizations merely because names, phones or addresses resemble one another. Keep neighboring cities outside local counts.

## Insurance signals and possible future badges

Explicit nonparticipation is useful information. Record Medi-Cal Dental, Medicare, specific HMO/PPO networks and new-patient capacity separately. No HMO is not no Medi-Cal; no Medi-Cal is not private-insurance-only, and participation is not open appointment capacity. Never infer an office's motives, frustration, patient preference or care quality from an insurance statement.

A possible future patient-facing badge is **No Medi-Cal — practice-stated**, with the source, checked date and exact location available beside it. An HMO badge must independently reflect explicit HMO evidence. Color is supplemental: readable text and an accessible explanation carry the meaning. Avoid punitive styling or ranking penalties.

A badge can be considered only after the explicit statement is read in its primary source and matched to the correct practice/location. Snippet-only, historical, missing or conflicting evidence stays in this log until resolved. A practice's statement is attributed, not an independent government enrollment determination. The eventual UI needs corrections/refresh handling and must not imply permanence. This document authorizes research storage; it does not deploy badges or change indexing/review states.

## Reuse boundaries

Preserve enough provenance to reduce repeated work. Summarize rather than archive complete copyrighted pages. Do not store patient details, credentials, private communications or personal speculation. Photo leads retain the physician/practice-owned asset requirement and unresolved use/privacy states. Logs never clear assets automatically.
